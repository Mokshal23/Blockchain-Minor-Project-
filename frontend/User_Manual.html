First-Time User Manual

Dual-Engine Blockchain Crowdfunding Platform

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to the Dual-Engine Blockchain Crowdfunding Platform. This platform provides two distinct crowdfunding architecture engines designed for different funding models:

Funding Model Assigned Engine Prerequisite Key Feature
Donation Python Engine None (Cloud API) 0 Gas cost, instant SHA-256 block ledger, central admin approval.
Reward Python Engine None (Cloud API) Reward tier mapping (Bronze/Silver/Gold) based on contribution.
Equity Solidity Engine MetaMask Wallet Ethereum Smart Contract escrow, weighted contributor milestone voting.
Lending Solidity Engine MetaMask Wallet Debt repayment pool, pull-based lender withdrawals.

2. Quick Start: Setting Up MetaMask (For Equity & Lending)

If you only plan to test Donation or Reward campaigns, you can skip this step! If you want to test Equity or Lending campaigns, follow these quick steps:

1Install MetaMask Extension

Install the MetaMask Browser Extension in Chrome, Brave, Edge, or Firefox.

2Connect Wallet

Click the Connect MetaMask button at the top right of the navbar. Select your account and approve connection.

Network Auto-Switching: The platform will automatically prompt MetaMask to connect to Sepolia Testnet or Hardhat Localhost (Chain ID: 31337 / 0x7a69).

3. Step-by-Step Usage Guides

Guide A: Creating a New Campaign

1 Click the Create Campaign tab in the navigation bar.
2 Fill in Campaign Title, Description, Funding Goal (in ETH/USD), and Duration.
3 Select the Funding Model:
  • Selecting Donation or Reward routes to the Python Engine.
  • Selecting Equity or Lending routes to the Solidity Engine.
4 Click Launch Campaign. For Solidity models, confirm the transaction prompt in MetaMask.

Guide B: Contributing to a Campaign

1 Navigate to the Browse Campaigns tab.
2 Locate the campaign card you want to back.
3 Enter the amount (e.g. 1.0) in the inline input box directly on the card.
4 Click Contribute Now.
  • Python Engine: Instantly updates raised balance and appends a SHA-256 block.
  • Solidity Engine: MetaMask opens to confirm on-chain deposit into Smart Contract Escrow. Once confirmed, raised progress updates live.

Guide C: Voting on Solidity Milestones (Equity & Lending)

Voting Rule: You must contribute to an Equity or Lending campaign first before you can vote! Your voting weight equals your contribution amount.
1 Inspect the Milestone 1 Voting Breakdown box on an Equity or Lending card.
2 Check your status badge (should display Eligible Voter).
3 Click Vote YES or Vote NO and confirm in MetaMask.
4 If YES votes exceed 50% of raised funds, the Smart Contract auto-releases 50% of funds to the creator!

Guide D: Lending Repayments & Pull Withdrawals

1 Borrower Deposit: Click Deposit Repayment on a Lending card to deposit funds into the contract pool.
2 Lender Withdrawal: Lenders see their live Claimable Repayment amount. Click Pull Repayment to withdraw your entitled principal + interest directly into your wallet.

Guide E: Deleting a Campaign

1 Click Delete Campaign at the bottom of any campaign card.
2 Confirm the prompt. The card will be permanently deleted from the database/interface.

4. Admin Panel & SHA-256 Tamper Demonstration

To demonstrate why centralized ledgers are vulnerable compared to decentralized smart contracts:

1 Open the Admin Panel (Python Engine) tab.
2 Click Run Admin Tamper Script.
3 Observe the side-by-side comparison showing historical block data modified from $100 to $1,000, while the chain status still reports VALID because the central admin recalculated all hashes.
